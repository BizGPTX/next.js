## Overview

Electrician GPT is a specialized assistant designed for electricians working in Australia. It combines award interpretation, safety compliance research, estimating workflows, and electrical knowledge to help apprentices, qualified electricians, and trade assistants make informed decisions quickly.

## Award Wages and Industrial Instruments

- Surfaces current information from the **Electrical, Electronic and Communications Contracting Award** and relevant enterprise agreements, including minimum hourly rates, allowances, and overtime rules for apprentices and qualified tradespeople.
- Highlights key Fair Work Commission resources so users can confirm classification levels, competency stages, and state-based variations before issuing payslips or quotes.
- Guides trade assistants through eligibility questions (e.g. experience, licensing, region) to point them to the correct pay tables.

## Work Health and Safety Compliance

- Summarizes the latest Work Health and Safety (WHS) regulations, Safe Work Australia model Codes of Practice, and state-specific electrical safety rules.
- Surfaces mandatory documentation such as Safe Work Method Statements (SWMS), isolation procedures, testing requirements, and personal protective equipment (PPE) checklists for common tasks.
- Provides reminders about licensing obligations, incident notification thresholds, and record-keeping expectations to reduce compliance risk on site.

## Estimating, Quoting, and Measurement Support

- Helps scope residential, commercial, and industrial jobs by collecting load schedules, circuit counts, cabling routes, and switchboard requirements.
- Generates bill of materials templates that include cable lengths, conduit sizes, fixings, hardware, and labour allowances with adjustable margins.
- Suggests time-and-material breakdowns, optional upgrades, and exclusions for quotes so clients receive transparent proposals.

## Technical Questions and Standards

- References the Australian/New Zealand Wiring Rules (**AS/NZS 3000**), state service and installation rules, and relevant building codes when answering design or installation questions.
- Offers step-by-step troubleshooting guidance for protective devices, earthing, fault-finding, and testing procedures while reminding users to verify against official standards.
- Provides links to product datasheets, distributor catalogs, and manufacturer installation instructions for compliant component selection.

## Responsible Usage

Electrician GPT complements—not replaces—professional judgment. Always verify award data against the latest Fair Work determinations, confirm compliance requirements with the regulator in your jurisdiction, and have licensed electricians sign off on electrical work. When in doubt, consult primary legislation and certified industry advisers before acting.
